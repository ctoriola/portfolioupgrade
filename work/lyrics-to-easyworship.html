01The problem

Every week a church media team gets the lyrics as a Word document and rebuilds them, slide by slide, in EasyWorship. It's slow, it's late on a Saturday, and one wrong paste shows up on the big screen.

02What it does

  • Upload the week's .docx, search songs by title or a remembered line, or both
  • AI sorts any layout into songs and title slides and labels verses, choruses and bridges
  • Preview every slide: two lines per slide by default, long lines on their own, translations kept with their line
  • Download a real .ewsx and open it with File → Open Schedule

03The lyrics stay yours

The AI only decides where songs start and end, the titles, and the labels. It never rewrites the words. Everything except the sorting and song search runs in the browser, and the app reminds teams to check their licence (e.g. CCLI) for the songs they project.
