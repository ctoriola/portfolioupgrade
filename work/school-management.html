01The work

School admin software where staff manage students, classes and results. It's the kind of system that has to be right every morning, because a whole school opens it to start the day.

02What it taught me

Building for schools meant building for real users with real deadlines: result days, new terms, a full intake. It's where I learned that reliability is a feature, and it's the reason I still build end to end.

03Try the demo

The live demo runs a sample school with one-click sign-ins for every role: proprietor, principal, school admin, bursar, teachers and parents. Students, attendance, results and fees sit in one place, and parents are kept in the loop by SMS and WhatsApp.
