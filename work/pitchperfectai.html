01The problem

  • Great products get lost in weak, unstructured pitches.
  • Writing and designing a deck takes days founders don't have.
  • Technical teams struggle to turn code into a business story.

02What it does

Describe your startup in a sentence or two, or paste a GitHub repository, public or private. It reads the README, topics and languages, and turns those code signals into business value. With no repo, it builds the story from your description plus market context from a quick web search.

You choose the length, 4, 6 or 10 slides, and it writes one slide per section, grounding product claims in what you gave it. Then you download a styled PowerPoint.

03Pitchy, the coach

Pitchy scores every section out of five, tells you what investors will find weak, starting with the weakest, and rewrites it with one click. Keep the change or undo it.

04How it's built

A Flask backend with Gemini drafting each slide. The deck builder splits content into slide-sized sections, the same approach I later reused in PDF → Slides.
