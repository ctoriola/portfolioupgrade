01The idea

Most file sharing asks you to trust the server with your files. NCrypt doesn't: contracts, payroll sheets and brand assets are encrypted on your own device first, so what gets uploaded is ciphertext the server can't read.

02How it works

  • Encrypted on your device. AES-256-GCM with a key derived from your passphrase (PBKDF2, 100,000 rounds). Nothing readable leaves the browser.
  • Ciphertext goes to storage. The encrypted bytes go straight to the storage bucket. The server never sees the file or the passphrase.
  • Share the ID, not the key. You get an 8-character share ID. Send it and the passphrase separately; the recipient decrypts in their own browser.

03How it's built

A React front end on the browser's built-in Web Crypto API. No crypto libraries to ship or trust, and the plaintext never leaves the page.
