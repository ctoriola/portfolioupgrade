Case study · Brand overhaul

PrimeTEK SSC

A complete brand overhaul for PrimeTEK Safety & Security Consultants, a Nigerian defence consultancy: precise, discreet and mission-focused.

Client
PrimeTEK Safety & Security Consultants
Sector
Defence and security consultancy
Scope
Brand identity, visual system, website
Live
primetekssc.com

01The brief

PrimeTEK SSC works with national agencies and critical infrastructure on military solutions, intelligence, cyber defence and risk. The audience is senior, careful and short on time.

The brand had to signal capability and discipline in seconds, without falling back on the usual padlocks and glowing circuit boards.

02The system

A near-black canvas with one safety-orange accent. “Safety & Security Consultants.” is set in outlined type, with a rotating “Since 2012 · Defence Solutions” seal and the years of experience front and centre.

Section labels sit in braces ({Core capabilities}, {Our standards}), and the standards panel reads like a mission console: “PTK‑SSC // Operational standards · All systems nominal.” A giant PRIMETEK wordmark anchors the footer.

04Voice

Mission-aligned solutions for complex threat environments.

Measured and exact. Claims are backed by structure rather than adjectives: “Uncompromising on every front. Every PrimeTEK engagement is held to the same bar, without exception.”
