01The problem

Small businesses track invoices in one place, expenses in another and VAT in a spreadsheet, and often only find out what they owe close to the deadline.

02Built around Nigerian rules

Not a generic accounting app with a currency switch. It's built on the rules SMEs actually file against:

  • 7.5% VAT, worked out as each sale and cost is recorded, with VAT-free items flagged
  • Returns due on the 21st, with a countdown to the next one
  • TINs on invoices, part payments, and expenses approved in one tap
  • Naira first, Lagos time

03An assistant that doesn't make things up

Smart insights flag overdue invoices and unfiled returns, and an AI assistant explains the figures without inventing any. Monthly VAT reports export to CSV for the return itself.

The app is upfront that its figures are estimates to plan with, not tax advice.
