Case study · Brand overhaul

Hengtai Raytech

A complete brand overhaul for Hengtai Raytech, a Nigerian defence technology maker: one blue, orbital visual system for a company moving from supplier to manufacturer.

Client
Hengtai Raytech
Sector
Defence technology: SAR surveillance, satellites, robotics
Scope
Logo and identity, brand guidelines, print and collateral, website design and build
Live
hengtairaytech.com

01The brief

Hengtai Raytech began as an equipment supplier and has repositioned as an original manufacturer, building inside Nigeria's defence industrial ecosystem. It makes MiniSAR radar payloads for drones, the SmartSat-X satellite payload, and the X30 and M20 ground robots.

The brand had to carry that shift, from supplier to sovereign manufacturer: technology designed, built and kept in Nigeria.

02The system

One colour world. Every image, from Earth seen from orbit to ground station antennas, SAR imagery and the robots themselves, is washed in a single blue duotone, so space, air and ground read as one capability.

A tight grotesk sets short, heavy headlines, and the whole site runs on one three-part idea: See. Know. Act. Specs are given the weight of headlines (0.1 m imaging, 30 km+ links, IP67), and a giant Hengtai Raytech wordmark closes every page.

04Voice

Capability, owned.

Short and certain, built around ownership: “Build here. Keep it here.”, “Built for the terrain. Built to stay.” and “Intelligence that survives the weather.”

05The result

Hengtai Raytech was already doing Tier-1 work. The overhaul gives it a brand that presents it as the manufacturer it has become, consistently, from the website to print.
