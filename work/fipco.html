Case study · Brand overhaul

FIPCO

A complete brand overhaul for First Interstate Petrochemicals: an industrial, type-led identity that reads as big as the company it represents.

Client
First Interstate Petrochemicals Ltd.
Sector
Petrochemicals, energy logistics, oil & gas
Scope
Brand identity, visual system, website
Live
fipcoltd.com

01The brief

FIPCO works across petrochemical supply, energy logistics, industrial consulting and oil exploration, from offices in Lagos, Abuja and Akwa Ibom. One brand had to speak to manufacturers, energy partners and government stakeholders at the same time.

It needed to feel like heavy industry: confident, solid and serious, not another corporate template with a stock photo of a handshake.

02The system

Type does the heavy lifting. The FIPCO wordmark runs edge to edge with an offshore rig breaking through the letters, so the name and the work arrive together.

Headlines are uppercase and split into two tones, a white statement and a grey follow-through: “Energy solutions for a nation / on the move.” A charcoal base, one signal red reserved for action, numbered sections (01 / About us) and a running ticker of service lines keep long pages in order.

04Voice

Energy. Industry. Progress.

Short, declarative lines throughout: “No shortcuts. Just commitment.” and “Power your next move.” The three values, reliability, safety and partnership, each get one sentence and no padding.

05Where it lives

The website is where the whole system comes together: hero, services, standards, leadership, offices, FAQ, and a full-bleed red call to action to close.
